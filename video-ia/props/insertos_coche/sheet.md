# INSERTOS DEL COCHE — llave, cuadro, rueda, escape, volante

**Video:** `on_the_road` · **Planos:** 7, 8, 12, 17, 18, 22, 23, 26, 27
Nueve planos, 9.4 segundos, casi un cuarto del video.

Todos se generan **con la referencia del coche adjunta**, para que el material coincida.

## 1. Llave de contacto (plano 7)

```
HERO PROP — IGNITION KEY: a worn brass key on a plain split ring, no fob, no tag,
inserted into a chromed ignition barrel set into a matte black dash panel.
```
```
[ESTILO] [PROP COCHE] [PROP LLAVE]
Extreme close-up, a dark-skinned hand turning the key in the barrel, the yellow
#F2BB34 body panel out of focus behind, hard warm side light, shallow depth of field.
The face of the character is NOT in frame. 16:9.
```
> `The face of the character is NOT in frame` va escrito a proposito. Sin esa frase, los
> modelos meten media cara en el borde del cuadro con una frecuencia sorprendente.

## 2. Cuadro de instrumentos (plano 8) y velocimetro (plano 26)

```
HERO PROP — GAUGE CLUSTER: three round analogue dials in a matte black binnacle
behind a chromed bezel, white needles, cream numerals on black faces, the centre
dial the largest. Glass slightly scratched, dust in the bezel groove.
NEVER: digital displays with readable words, brand names on the dial faces.
```
```
[ESTILO] [PROP CUADRO]
Extreme close-up of the centre dial filling two thirds of frame, the needle swung
past the middle of its arc, the outer dials cut by the frame edges, hard reflection
across the glass, warm interior light from camera-left. 16:9.
```
Para el plano 26 se repite cambiando `the needle swung past the middle` por
`the needle near the end of its arc`. **Es el mismo prop y tiene que verse igual.**

## 3. Rueda (planos 12, 23, 27)

```
HERO PROP — WHEEL: a deep-dish polished five-spoke alloy wheel, low-profile tyre
with a worn shoulder, a riveted steel arch extension above it in cadmium yellow
#F2BB34, brake dust on the spoke faces.
```
Tres encuadres distintos del mismo objeto:

| Plano | Prompt de encuadre |
|---|---|
| 12 | `Low close-up from ground level, the wheel static, white tyre smoke drifting across frame, a light pole and sky behind. 16:9.` |
| 23 | `Very low tracking close-up alongside the moving wheel, the spokes blurred into a disc, asphalt streaking underneath, a pickup out of focus ahead. 16:9.` |
| 27 | `Low backlit close-up, the low sun directly behind the wheel, the spokes reading as a dark silhouette against flare, dust in the light. 16:9.` |

**El objeto no cambia; cambian la camara y la luz.** Esa es la diferencia entre tres planos de
la misma rueda y tres ruedas distintas.

## 4. Escapes (plano 18)

```
HERO PROP — EXHAUST TIPS: two chromed exhaust tips side by side under a matte black
rear valance, heat-blued at the mouth, soot inside.
```
```
[ESTILO] [PROP ESCAPES]
Close-up from directly behind and low, a short blue flame flaring from the right tip,
the yellow rear plate out of focus above, everything else in shadow. 16:9.
```
> Esta llama azul `#4A9FD8` es **el unico color frio de todo el tercer acto**. En un video de
> 15 segundos de naranja, un frio de 0.87 s es lo que evita que el ojo se sature. Cuando montes
> tu propio video, busca deliberadamente donde meter el tuyo.

## 5. Volante (plano 22)

```
HERO PROP — STEERING WHEEL: a three-spoke wheel with a polished aluminium centre,
drilled spokes, a thin dark wood rim worn pale where hands sit.
```
```
[ESTILO] [PROP VOLANTE]
Close-up of two dark-skinned hands at ten and two on the rim, a black leather watch
on the left wrist, the gauge cluster out of focus beyond, warm light from the
windscreen. The face is NOT in frame. 16:9.
```
El reloj esta en el bloque de identidad del personaje. **Repetirlo aqui es lo que hace que
el espectador sepa de quien son las manos** sin ver la cara.

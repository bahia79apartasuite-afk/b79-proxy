## Descripcion inmutable

```
LOCATION — NIGHT STREET: a run-down commercial street after midnight. Roll-down
steel shutters covered in tags, a short flight of concrete steps up to a doorway, a
row of abandoned shopping trolleys against the kerb, a steel scaffold and fire escape
climbing the building above. Wet tarmac. Two light sources and no others: a red neon
sign camera-right throwing signal red #E03D26 across every face and surface it can
reach, and the cold blue #215E91 of distant city windows filling everything it cannot.
No white light anywhere. Halation bleeding around the neon.
```

## Variantes de hora

Esta ubicacion **solo existe de noche** en el video, y ese es un dato de estilo, no una
limitacion. Pero si la necesitas de dia:

| Variante | Que cambiar |
|---|---|
| Noche (la del video) | `red neon and blue city light only, no white light anywhere` |
| Atardecer | `last warm daylight #C4703A from street level, the neon just switched on and barely reading` |
| Dia | `flat grey daylight, the neon off and reading as a dead grey tube, the street stripped of all its colour` |

> La variante de dia es util aunque no la uses: **generarla te ensena cuanto de la ubicacion
> es geometria y cuanto es luz.** En esta, casi todo es luz.
